Analytical Method

For a point (x0, y0) and the parabola f(x) = x2 + 5, the squared distance is

D(x) = (x - x0)2 + (x2 + 5 - y0)2.

Differentiating and setting the derivative equal to zero gives

D'(x) = 2(x - x0) + 4x(x2 + 5 - y0) = 0.
